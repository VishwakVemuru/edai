edai: 300 problems

The edai brain answers 300 small problems here, on this device, with no network: 120 hidden rules (both answers must be exact), 120 noisy trends (both estimates within the tolerance) and 60 sets of pure noise (it must answer CANNOT). On the computers tried so far it scored 275.
